# The number that ended four rounds of guessing

**Date:** 2026-08-30

The diagnostic line added the day before did its job on the first look:

> **Focus check:** read 8 of 8. Softest reading 2.23 (anything under 0.4 is offered above).

Eight of eight photographs read. The check was working perfectly. **The softest photograph in
the album — the visibly blurred one — read 2.23 against a threshold of 0.4.** Five times over
the line, and every absolute threshold tried in four rounds had been drawn from scenes that
read around 1.

## Why the scale was wrong, and why no synthetic scene could have shown it

The reading is detail divided by contrast. How much fine detail a photograph carries is a
fact about the **scene**, not about the focus: water, sand, foliage and hair produce far more
of it than a wall or a sky. Fractal texture built to stand in for "a natural photograph"
produced readings around 1 when sharp. A real album of beach photographs — rippling shallow
water, wet sand, spray — produced 2 to 15, with the blurred frame still at 2.23.

So a blurred real photograph reads higher than a sharp synthetic one. No amount of care in
building those scenes would have found this; only a real album could, which is exactly what
the diagnostic line was for.

## What replaces it

Photographs are now judged **against their own album**: the median reading of everything
measured there, and anything under three fifths of it is offered.

Within one album the comparison holds — the same camera, the same sort of subject, mostly the
same afternoon — where absolute numbers do not. In the album this was calibrated against, the
sharp photographs sit near the median and the blurred one sits far below it.

Two guards around it:

- **An album needs at least four measured photographs** before a median means anything. The
  softest of three is not evidence.
- **The old absolute floor stays underneath**, for the frame that is blurred beyond argument
  and for albums too small to compare within. It is no longer the main rule, which was the
  mistake, but a reading that low means nothing in the frame has an edge.

## The diagnostic line now lists every reading

Not just the softest, and it names the line the album settled on. If three fifths turns out
to be the wrong share, the next look supplies the exact numbers to set it from rather than
another round of inference.

## What this cost, and what it should have been

Four rounds, three merges, and a threshold argued from evidence that could not settle it.
Every one of those rounds was spent making the measurement more correct — and the measurement
was never the problem after the second round. The problem was that nothing in the product
could tell the owner's report ("still not showing") apart from a dozen different causes, and
the fix for that was one line of text.

**Build the thing that makes the report legible before building the third fix for a cause you
cannot observe.**
